## Good pairings on Midori MD cream paper

- Lamy 2000 14k EF inked with Taccia Sabimidori (cursive)
- Lamy 2000 14k F inked with Sailor Rikyucha (cursive)
- Lamy 2000 14k M inked with Sailor Rikyucha (cursive and print)
- Lamy 2000 14k OM inked with Diamine Writer’s Blood (cursive and print)
- Lamy 2000 14k OM inked with Sailor  Okuyama (cursive and print)
- Pilot Capless 18k M inked with Pilot Momiji (print)
- Pilot Capless 18k M inked with Pilot Syo-Ro (print)
- Kaweco Sport steel B inked with Kaweco Summer Purple (print)
- Sailor Pro Gear 21k MF inked with Sailor Tokiwa-Matsu (cursive)

## Good pairings on Tomoe River 52 gsm cream/white paper

- Pilot Capless 18k M inked with Pilot Take-Sumi (cursive)
- Pilot Capless 18k M inked with Pilot Tsuki-Yo (cursive)
- Pilot Capless 18k M inked with Pilot Momiji (cursive and print)
- Lamy 2000 14k M inked with Pilot Kon-Peki (cursive)
- Lamy 2000 14k OBB inked with Pilot Yama-Budo (cursive and print)
- Kaweco Sport steel B inked with Kaweco Summer Purple (print)

## Bad pairings on Midori MD cream paper

- Lamy 2000 14k F inked with Sailor Doyou (don't like it)
- Lamy 2000 14k M inked with Pilot Momiji (too light, no sheen)
- Lamy 2000 14k M inked with Pilot Kon-Peki (too wet)
- Lamy 2000 14k OBB inked with Pilot Yama-Budo (too dry, no sheen)
- Sailor Pro Gear 21k B inked with Sailor Shimoyo (too dry)
- Pilot Capless 18k M inked with Sailor Yonaga (too wet)
- Pilot Kakuno steel M inked with Pilot Tsuki-Yo (too dry, no shading, no sheen)
